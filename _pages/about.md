I am a researcher at [Know Center Research GmbH](https://www.know-center.at/en/). My research focuses on privacy-preserving and trustworthy data analysis and machine learning, with a particular emphasis on differential privacy. I am especially interested in the trade-offs and synergies between different requirements for trustworthy AI (in particular privacy and fairness), as well as the practical and societal implications of integrating differential privacy into real-world systems.

I recently defended my PhD thesis on implications of privacy-preserving methods in data analytics and machine learning at the [University of Technology, Graz (Austria)](https://www.tugraz.at/en/home/) under supervision of [Andreas Trügler](https://atruegler.at/).
